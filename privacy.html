2. What DealSwipe is

DealSwipe helps a household with its weekly shop: you keep a shared shopping list, DealSwipe compares it with supermarket flyer offers, and you tick things off together while shopping. The app is invite-only: we create the accounts, there is no public sign-up.

3. What data we process

Account

Email address and password (stored only as a hash), plus technical sign-in data such as the time of your last sign-in. Purpose: so you can sign in and your data belongs only to you and your household.

Profile

Display name, an optional profile photo, your default shop and your language. The other members of your household see your name, email address and profile photo.

Household data

Everything you enter belongs to your household and is visible to all its members: the main list (products, brands, usual prices and shops), the weekly list, your decisions on offers, ticked-off items and who ticked them, finished shops, your own recipes and favourites, and a changelog ("Anna added milk").

Receipts

When you photograph a receipt or pick one from your library, we upload the photo to private storage. It is read on a computer of the controller in Germany by a locally running AI model; the photo is not sent to any external AI provider. We keep the recognised lines (products, prices, shop, date, total) to work out usual prices. The photo itself is deleted after 30 days; the lines stay until you delete the receipt or your account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token and your language. We use them to tell you when someone in your household changes the weekly list or has been shopping, when your receipt has been read, and when new offers match your list. For the offers notice we compare the households' main lists with the new offers once a day (only list entries are processed, no names or email addresses).

On your device

The app keeps a copy of your data and your settings on the device so it is fast and works offline. The widget and Live Activity show your shop on the lock screen; that data does not leave your device for this. The app only uses the camera and photos when you choose a receipt or a profile photo yourself.

Server logs

Requests to our server produce technical data (such as IP address, time, requested function). Our hosting provider keeps it briefly for operations and security.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party analytics or crash tools, no sharing or selling to third parties. Offers come from the supermarkets' public flyers and contain no personal data.

4. Legal bases

  • Account, profile, household data, receipts and offer matching: to provide the app you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Notifications: your consent, given through the iOS prompt and revocable at any time in the app or in iOS Settings (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Server logs: our legitimate interest in secure, working operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Service providers and location

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA) runs our database, sign-in and file storage. The data is stored on servers in London (United Kingdom), which has an EU adequacy decision.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) forwards notifications to Apple; for that, Expo receives the push token and the notification text.
  • Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) delivers notifications through the Apple Push Notification service and distributes the app.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) runs the daily flyer import and the comparison with the main lists. No data is stored at GitHub permanently.

Data processing agreements or the providers' privacy terms apply. Where data goes to the USA, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) where the provider is certified under it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Household data: as long as the household exists. If you delete your account and are alone in the household, the whole household is deleted. If there are other members, the list, receipts and recipes stay for them; the note that you added something is removed.
  • Receipt photos: 30 days. Recognised lines: until you delete the receipt or the household.
  • Push tokens: until you turn notifications off, sign out or delete your account.
  • Server logs: a few days, as set by our hosting provider.

7. Deleting your account

In the app under Profile → Delete account. Your login, profile, profile photo and push tokens are deleted right away, as described in section 6. You can also just email us.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Just email us.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Baden-Württemberg data protection commissioner (baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de).
